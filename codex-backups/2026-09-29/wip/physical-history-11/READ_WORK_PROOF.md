# Finite work accounting — source proof only

Let N be the complete original Operations node count plus the set union of the exact retained publication temporary/final paths not already in P. Counts use checked arithmetic. Original directory modes and roles come from immutable P. There is no new directory producer in this closed engine. Depth is at most64 relative to Operations (Scratch/Ingress roots start at1). Existing baseline original bounds remain unchanged; this source does not solve producer payload/metadata exceeding that representation.

Let U be positively authenticated original unique physical payload plus the sum of the exact retained producer byte recipes. Both aliases are visited/counted/proved. Before any accepted image escapes, exact typed pair proof is mandatory; the scanner's conservative own namespace stream envelope is2U because an admitted payload can have at most two aliases. That2U is not a whole-call or whole-effect work bound. Every raw read is64KiB or less with a single EOF probe. Positive short reads each advance at least one byte; a successful scan has at most streamed namespace bytes + regular-file count pread calls (including EOF). EINTR/error refuses instead of unbounded retry. Requested/returned payload counters, cursor calls and node visits use checked arithmetic.

For each directory with at most M admitted members, one cursor has at most M+3 actual readdir calls, including dot entries and EOF, with an explicit checked bound before every call. A repeated dot/name cannot create an unbounded loop. Duplicate/undecodable names refuse. Each selected directory is enumerated twice per raw scan; Operations membership twice. Thus successful cursor work is bounded by2*(N-R+3D)+2*(Mops+3), where R is present selected roots and D their selected directory count. PFP own ancestor/policy operations and outside cursor work are separate.

One observe() performs two complete raw selected scans, two outside checks, and one genuine policy pass. captureInitial/requireImage each perform two observe calls: four raw scans, four outside checks, two policy passes. willPerform plus didPerform therefore perform eight raw selected scans, eight outside checks and four policy passes. Each policy pass invokes PFP once per positively selected pair and once per accepted singleton/directory. The pair helper performs four payload SHA streams per invocation. It is not invoked once per alias. Unaccepted active temporary nodes have no qualified policy until the real setter settles.

For one stable opaque pair of payload B, selected payload per complete effect boundary is raw8*(2B)=16B plus pair PFP4*(4B)=16B, total32B; this excludes outside scans, source/catalog reads, pure issuer/frame work, private metadata/Foundation internals and any source helper. Raw and PFP EOF requests add32 one-byte probes even when EOF returns0. A new link boundary is before4 raw single-alias plus after4 raw double-alias=12B, plus after2 pair policy calls*4B=8B, total20B selected payload. The policy setter adds its own real2B pre/post streams. These are examples, not a global2B claim.

Catalog entry and exit each perform a complete requireImage proof, so the catalog has eight raw scans/eight outside checks/four policy passes independent of the number C of resource closes. Each close checks actual finite Ledger resource identity/state/sequence/outcome without a whole-image scan or fake afterImage. Canonical source bytes/policy/read work remains actual source work; every source resource is positively catalog-tagged before open and no source output escapes until the complete final proof and private all-resources checked-close census.

Outside checks retain the unchanged ad95 strict branch walker and original root/NotificationAfter bindings. Untouched Operations regular leaves are SHA-streamed rather than put in whole Data or narrowed to32MiB. Their payload/count/cursor work and the existing baseline walker error/retry behavior are separate and have not been converted into a new global work theorem. Actual admission/provider and Foundation work must be inventoried in the coherent family; no authority decision is cached to make scans faster. Full effect performance remains subject to measured verification and may require a separately reviewed bounded window.

Stored dictionaries are built once per scan rather than repeatedly per selected node. Directory-to-original-source mappings are DATA advanced only by the exact committed rename effect; they do not cache authority. Retained source bytes and publisher recipes are finite lossless control DATA, not opaque payload copies. Array/control representation and complete producer-range obligations remain explicit.
